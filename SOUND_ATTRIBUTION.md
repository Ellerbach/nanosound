# Sound sources

## Farm sounds

The files in `sd-card/02` are adapted from freely reusable field recordings on
Wikimedia Commons. Each recording retains its original duration and is not
repeated. The files are loudness-normalized, mixed to mono, resampled to 44.1
kHz, and encoded as 128 kbit/s MP3 files.
